Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Cocktail Publisher ("the Service", "we", "us") explains in this policy what information we collect when you connect your TikTok account to the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login, we receive only the data you authorize:

  • Basic profile information: your TikTok display name (nickname), avatar and account identifier (scope: user.info.basic).
  • An access token and refresh token that allow the Service to publish or upload content to your account on your instruction (scopes: video.publish, video.upload).
  • Content you choose to publish through the Service: video or photo files, captions, hashtags and post settings (privacy level, comment/duet/stitch settings, commercial content disclosure).
  • Post status information returned by TikTok, such as whether a post was processed successfully.

We do not collect your TikTok password, and we do not access your messages, followers, contacts or any data beyond the permissions listed above.

2. How we use your information

  • To show you which TikTok account you are posting to.
  • To publish or upload content to your TikTok account, only when you confirm the action.
  • To show you the status of your posts.
  • To maintain the security and proper operation of the Service.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as described below.

3. Sharing of information

  • TikTok: content and post settings are sent to TikTok through the official TikTok Content Posting API to complete the actions you request. TikTok's handling of your data is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers: we may use hosting and infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data storage and retention

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored securely and used only to perform actions you request.
  • Uploaded media files are kept only as long as needed to complete publishing, then deleted from our systems.
  • Profile information and tokens are deleted when you disconnect your account or request deletion.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect at any time: revoke the Service's access from your TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Delete your data: email aiprojects@reedteams.com and we will delete your stored data within 30 days.
  • You may request a copy of the information we hold about you.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to credentials, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, contact aiprojects@reedteams.com.
