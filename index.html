Cocktail Publisher

Cocktail Publisher is a web dashboard that lets creators plan, schedule and publish their videos to TikTok, with full control over captions, privacy level and interaction settings.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account securely with TikTok Login.
  2. Select a video, write or edit the caption, and choose the privacy level and comment, duet and stitch settings.
  3. Disclose commercial content if applicable and confirm. Your post is published through the official TikTok Content Posting API.
  4. Track the processing status of each post. It may take a few minutes for content to appear on your profile.

You can disconnect at any time from your TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
